Frequently asked questions

Can I use these without engaging you?

Yes. The sample databook and the data-request list are published for anyone to use. If you would like us to populate them for your deal, that is the first step of an engagement.

What is the difference between the sample and a live deliverable?

The sample is built on synthetic data and published in full. A live deliverable is built on the target's actual data, under NDA, and the working Excel file is delivered only to the client.
