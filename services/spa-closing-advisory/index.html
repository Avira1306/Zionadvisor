SPA & Closing Advisory

The financial mechanics inside the sale agreement — completion accounts, locked box, and the schedules that decide the final price.

Discuss a deal Engagement models

Where value leaks

A deal can be agreed at a headline price and settled at a materially different one. The difference usually sits in the completion mechanism drafted into the SPA.

  • Completion accounts review. Testing the accounts prepared at closing against the agreed accounting policies.
  • Locked box analysis. Reviewing the reference balance sheet and testing for leakage between the locked box date and completion.
  • Definition support. Reviewing net debt, working capital and cash definitions before they are signed rather than after.
  • Adjustment schedules. Building the calculation that supports your position, tied to source data.

Outputs you receive

  • Commentary on the financial definitions in the draft SPA, flagging ambiguity before signature.
  • Completion accounts or locked box review with a variance schedule against the agreed basis.
  • Purchase price adjustment calculation with full supporting workings.
  • A short memo setting out our position, the counterparty's, and where the gap actually is.

Frequently asked questions

Do you draft the SPA?

No — that is your legal adviser's role. We review and support the financial definitions and schedules inside it, and we work alongside your lawyers.

Completion accounts or locked box — which is better?

It depends on the quality of the target's monthly reporting and the gap between signing and completion. We will give you a view on which mechanism protects you in the specific deal.

Can you help after a dispute has arisen?

Yes. We prepare the supporting analysis and schedules for your position, including working alongside an expert determination process.
