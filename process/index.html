Our Process

A rigorous and transparent workflow designed to deliver precise, audit-ready financial insights with utmost confidentiality.

Our Five-Stage Approach

1. Scope & Intake

Initial consultation to understand your specific needs, project objectives, and desired outcomes. We define the scope and gather preliminary information.

2. NDA & Secure Data Exchange

Confidentiality is our priority. We execute a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and establish secure channels for the exchange of sensitive financial data.

3. Build & Analysis

Our expert team undertakes the core work, performing in-depth analysis, building financial models, and preparing comprehensive databooks.

4. Senior Review

Every deliverable undergoes a rigorous senior review process to ensure accuracy, quality, and adherence to audit-grade standards.

5. Delivery & Debrief

We deliver the final reports and models, followed by a debrief session to discuss findings, answer questions, and provide strategic insights.

Accelerated Timelines: 72-Hour First Cut

For urgent due diligence needs, we offer a specialized 72-hour first-cut databook fast path. This accelerated process is designed for situations where rapid insights are critical, subject to agreed scope and timely data provision.

Frequently asked questions

What happens at kick-off?

We issue the data-request list, confirm the chart of accounts and reporting currency, and agree the diligence perimeter. If the target is Swedish, we ask for the SIE4 export at this stage.

What happens at the 72-hour mark?

You receive a first-cut databook: trial balance loaded, monthly P&L and balance sheet built, data gaps flagged. It is not a finished QoE; it is a first cut, and we say so.

What happens at delivery?

You receive the full databook, the reconciliation pack, the findings report and the open items log. Every figure is traceable to source, and every adjustment is evidenced.
