Why the Nordics are our priority market

It is the rare combination of real deal volume and unusually good source data — if you know how to read it.

  • The Nordics generate roughly 12–15% of European private equity deal volume by count, from a combined population of about 27 million — a persistently disproportionate share.
  • Sweden is the most active Nordic PE market, underpinned by technology, industrials, healthcare, consumer brands and export-oriented mid-market companies.
  • Stockholm hosts EQT (the largest Nordic-headquartered investor by enterprise value) and Nordic Capital, alongside mid-market houses including IK Partners, Adelis, Segulah and Axcel.
  • Nordic Capital closed its second dedicated mid-market fund, Evolution II, at a €2bn hard cap in December 2024 — capital specifically targeted at the deal size where outsourced diligence is common.
  • A deep base of owner-led businesses reaching succession means consistent lower-mid-market transaction flow, which is exactly where in-house diligence teams do not exist.

Pick a country

Each market has its own reporting basis, ledger export and statutory filing. The detail is what makes offshore support useful rather than extra work.

Sweden — priority market

SIE4 / SIE5 native · K2 / K3 bridging · Fortnox, Visma, Bokio

Our primary focus in the region. Full detail on SIE file types, where K2 and K3 diverge, and how we bridge onto a buyer’s basis.

Norway

SAF-T v1.30 · NGAAP · Tripletex, PowerOffice

Mandated standard audit file means transaction-level data is reliably available.

Denmark

Årsregnskabsloven · Reporting classes A–D · e-conomic, Dinero

Reporting class determines how much a buyer can learn before diligence starts.

Finland

FAS · PRH filings · Netvisor, Procountor

Euro-denominated, removing the translation layer present elsewhere in the region.
