Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Data we collect

  • Name, email, company, project details, and message content you submit through our contact and resource forms.
  • Basic, anonymous usage analytics (if enabled).

How we use data

To respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, deliver contracted services, and improve our website. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Confidentiality

Engagement data is handled under NDA-first confidentiality. Please do not send sensitive files through web forms; we will arrange a secure intake path.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email abhishek.bhandari@zionadvisor.com.

Your data

We do not sell, share or trade your personal data. We collect only what is required to respond to enquiries and to deliver engaged services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by writing to us.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses standard analytics to understand how the site is used. No personal information is collected through cookies, and we do not use third-party tracking for advertising purposes.

Data retention

We retain enquiry data for as long as is necessary to respond to your enquiry and to provide any services you engage us for. You may request deletion at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, write to us directly and we will respond within a reasonable period.
