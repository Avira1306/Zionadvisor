Frequently asked questions

Do you speak to buyers directly?

Only if you want us to. Default is that we sit behind you — you run all buyer contact and we turn the analysis around.

Can you work under our brand for the seller?

Yes. Deliverables carry your branding, and the seller need not know an offshore team was involved.

How do you handle Q&A volume during a live process?

We keep the databook live for the duration, so most buyer questions are answered by re-cutting existing schedules rather than starting fresh analysis.

Running a process this quarter?

We can have the vendor pack ready before you go to market.
