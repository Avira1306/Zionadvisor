Frequently asked questions

Do you work with firms smaller than the Big 4?

Yes. Most of our clients are boutiques and mid-market firms who need the capability of a large practice without the overhead. If you run transactions and need a delivery layer, we are the right size.

Can you work under our brand?

Yes. On white-label engagements we deliver in your templates under your branding. The client sees your firm, not ours.

What if we only need support on one deal?

That is most of our work. We price by project, with no minimum commitment. See our engagement models.
